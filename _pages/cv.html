I am a PhD candidate at TU Delft with a background in discrete mathematics, working at the intersection of applied mathematics, theoretical computer science, and phylogenetics. My research focuses on the mathematical and algorithmic foundations of phylogenetic networks, with particular emphasis on identifiability, network combinatorics, and algorithmic principles. In parallel, I develop open-source software and user interfaces that translate theoretical results into accessible tools for biological applications.
